Letta
the credit committee that never sleeps.
An autonomous invoice-factoring agent on Arc, settling in USDC.
Why factoring
SMEs ship the goods, then wait 60 days.
They have an invoice but no cash — can't make payroll, can't buy materials. Factoring advances the cash against the invoice.
But underwriting it is slow, manual, and closed to small suppliers. There's no stablecoin rail to do it programmatically.
How Letta works
An agent that prices risk with money at stake.
read on-chain history → Claude prices the advance → fund USDC on Arc → waterfall, re-assessed live
the decision is a judgment, not “approve a document I wrote” — so the autonomy is real
